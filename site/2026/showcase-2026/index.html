HXR Showcase & competition  2026

Hosted by the Harvard GSD XR Club, the Showcase brings together experimental projects, research prototypes, speculative futures, and applied XR products in a curated, interdisciplinary environment.

2026 Submission closed

The HXR Showcase & Competition 2026 invites creators, researchers, designers, and founders from around the world to present cutting-edge work at the intersection of XR, AI, spatial computing, and embodied media.

Under the 2026 theme From Pixel to Voxel, the showcase explores how immersive technologies are transforming design, perception, storytelling, and interaction—moving beyond screens toward fully embodied worlds.

Who Should Apply

We welcome submissions from individuals or teams working across XR, AI, and immersive media, including but not limited to:

- Artists & Designers

- Researchers & Students

- Technologists & Developers

- Startup Founders & Builders

Projects can be experimental, speculative, research-driven, or market-ready.

Submission Tracks

Applicants should choose one primary track based on the intent of their work.

Track 1: Exploratory / Research & Creative XR

For experimental, artistic, and research-driven projects, including:AI films and immersive narratives

- XR artworks and installations

- Research prototypes and speculative design

- Interactive media experiments

- Academic or conceptual XR work
‍
This track emphasizes ideas, exploration, and new modes of perception rather than commercial readiness.

Track 2: Applied / Venture & Product XR

For applied, product-oriented, or venture-backed projects, including:

- XR startups (pre-market or in-market)

- Developer platforms and tools

- Commercial or applied XR systems

- Products with validated user or partner interest

This track emphasizes real-world use, execution, and scalability.

Awards & Recognition 

Track-specific awards apply only to projects selected for the Pitch format. These awards recognize excellence within the curated group of teams invited to present their work on stage.
The Audience Choice Award is open to project selected and comfirmed to come to HXR Conference, regardless of track or format.

Awards — Exploratory / Research & Creative XR Track

Projects in the Exploratory / Creative XR Track are eligible for:

1. Best Exploratory XR Work

For outstanding conceptual, artistic, or research-driven XR projects

2. Best AI + XR Narrative

For excellence in storytelling, cinematic language, or narrative experimentation

Awards — Applied / Venture & Product XR Track

Projects in the Applied / Venture & Product XR Track are eligible for:

1. Best Applied XR Product

For strong execution, real-world relevance, and product clarity

2. Best Emerging XR Startup

For early-stage teams demonstrating exceptional promise and vision

Awards — Audience Choice Award

* The Audience Choice Award is open to project selected and comfirmed to come to HXR Conference, regardless of track or format.
‍
* Whether a team is presenting a Pitch or/and exhibiting a Demo Booth, all are equally eligible, and the winner is determined solely by the votes of HXR Conferene attendees.
‍
* The team that earns the strongest audience and guest vote across selected Showcase & Competition teams wins the award.
‍

What Winners & Finalist Receive

‍

‍

Selection Criteria

Track 1: Exploratory / Research & Creative XRProjects are evaluated on:Originality and innovation

- Conceptual clarity and intent

- Cultural, social, or research significance

- Exploration of XR as a medium

Track 2: Applied / Venture & Product XRProjects are evaluated using the Founders Lab Startup Framework, including:Team and presentation

- Problem–solution fit

- Product innovation

- Market and competitive positioning

- Real-world and social impact

Projects are evaluated within their track. There is no cross-track comparison.

What to Submit

A link that shows the best possible representation of your project in under 3 minutes.
Examples:AI film / XR film → link to the video

- AR glasses / hardware → link to a recorded demo of the product in use

- XR interactive game → link to a gameplay or walkthrough video

- VR film / immersive cinema → link to a screen capture or 360 preview

- Research or conceptual XR work → link to a short demo video or visual portfolio.

All material submitted should be in English or have English Subtitles.

Presentation Options (Only for Selected Projects)

Selected participants will be invited to present in one of the following formats:
* Selected project team will have a free all-access pass to HXR Conference.

1. Quick Pitch:

 A 3-minute presentation to the audience

2. Demo Booth:

 A dedicated table or space for live screening and interaction

3. Online Exhibition (Bonus):
