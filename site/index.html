Harvard GSD XR conference 2026: XR+: From Pixel to Voxel focuses on human-centered XR applications, aiming to empower the next generation of XR change-makers in today’s evolving industry. Our goal is to provide a platform for students, researchers, practitioners, and entrepreneurs to explore how XR is shaping the future across various industries. We will dive into the history, present, and future of XR, examining how emerging technologies can be leveraged and the challenges that lie ahead.
