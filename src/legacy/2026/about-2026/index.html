Founded in 2022, we are a group of grounded visionaries.

HXR
TEAM

We are a non-profit organization based out of Harvard that hosts annual conferences and explores the boundaries of XR, AI, experiential tech, and spatial computing.

Our goal is to provide a platform for students, researchers, practitioners, and entrepreneurs to discuss how digital realities are shaping the future across industries including entertainment, education, tech, and healthcare.
